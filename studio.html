Brunswick Recording Studios Archive (1916–1931)

This technical log maps the structural properties, matrix series locations, and studio facilities verified through the Discography of American Historical Recordings (DAHR).

🏢 The New York Master Facilities: 799 Seventh Avenue

The core nerve center of Brunswick's commercial output was meticulously documented across three distinct live rooms:

• April 1924: Brunswick opens its signature building at 799 Seventh Avenue.
• July 17, 1924: Room #2 is brought online, initially running its own master matrix series using a strict "B" prefix identifier.
• April 7, 1925: Room #3 is officially inaugurated to conduct experimental electrical recording testing using early prototypes of the Light-Ray Pallophotophone.
• May–June 1925: New York acoustic matrix cutting gradually winds down, shifting Room #1 and Room #2 entirely into specialized vacuum tube electrical lines by October 1925.
• December 21, 1931: Pre-ARC Brunswick cuts its final official historical master disk on site: Matrix E37525.

🏭 The Chicago Innovation Center: 666 Lake Shore Drive

Despite being the corporate headquarters, permanent technical tracking facilities weren't established in Chicago until the late-1950s boom era:

• 1923–1927: All early Chicago master recordings are captured using portable mechanical equipment sent by train from the New York laboratories.
• March 1928: Continuous electrical studio tracking is established directly inside the central Brunswick building.
• May–June 1929: Under the management of Jack Kapp, the entire studio operation is relocated to the 21st floor of the Furniture Mart at 666 Lake Shore Drive.
• October 1929: Studio A and Studio B are fully separated on the log books, deploying custom-built heavy lathes capable of mastering high-fidelity 16" and 18" radio transcriptions spinning at 33-1/3 RPM.

🌴 The West Coast Pipeline: Los Angeles & Remote Field Rips

The unique origin point of your label's explicit "Light-Ray Elec. Rec." stamp text:

• July 1923–May 1927: West Coast tracking relies entirely on portable mechanical layouts deployed during mobile field operations.
• Late 1926–Late 1927: Los Angeles regional pressing plants become the ONLY manufacturing facilities globally to stamp the physical legend "Light-Ray Elec. Rec." directly onto paper disc labels.
• December 1927: A permanent West Coast recording lab is finalized inside an active Brunswick warehouse at 2481 Porter Street, establishing the permanent LAE master tracking series.
